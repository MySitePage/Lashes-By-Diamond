Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions Answered

How long do lash extensions last?

Typically 4-6 weeks with proper aftercare. Your natural lashes shed, so fills are recommended every 2-3 weeks to maintain fullness.

Do lash extensions damage natural lashes?

No! When applied correctly by a certified artist with proper adhesive, they don't damage natural lashes. Aftercare is key.

What's the difference between classic and volume?

Classic uses a 1:1 ratio for natural elegance. Volume uses multiple thin fans for fuller, more dramatic look. Both beautiful!

Can I wear makeup with lash extensions?

Yes, but avoid oil-based makeup and oil cleansers. Water-based products are best. Never apply mascara directly on extensions.

What if I have sensitive eyes?

I offer patch tests for sensitive individuals and use hypoallergenic adhesive. Consult first if you have concerns!
